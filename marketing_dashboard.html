Google is the top-ranked platform Score 0.862

Despite Facebook having a marginally higher raw conversion rate (10.47% vs 10.35%), Google scores highest due to the largest conversion volume (498K), the lowest CPA ($24.19), and stronger efficiency per dollar. Google should receive the largest budget allocation.

Twitter is the weakest performer Score 0.006

Twitter has the lowest conversion rate (9.21%), fewest conversions (193K), and near-zero performance score despite $4.77M in spend. Shifting 25–30% of Twitter's budget to Google is the single highest-return budget action available.

Longer campaigns yield more conversions

Campaigns running over 12 months average 1,015 conversions vs 724 for campaigns under 30 days — a 40% advantage. Sustained always-on investment outperforms fragmented tactical short bursts for evergreen products and services.

H2 2024 activity decline Trend Alert

Campaign launches peaked at 174 in February 2024 and dropped 34% to 115 by October 2024, driving a proportional fall in monthly conversions ($4.27M → $2.95M spend). Proactive H2 planning and minimum-activity thresholds by platform are essential.

LinkedIn and Instagram are underutilised

Both platforms show solid ~10% conversion rates with 232–233 campaigns each, well below Google/Facebook (467–489). Controlled budget uplifts (+20%) on these channels could yield proportional conversion gains and portfolio diversification.

Zero-conversion campaigns waste budget

Three campaigns generated 0 conversions while spending up to $20,611 each. Twitter accounts for the majority of bottom-10 performers. An early-warning rule — pause campaigns with >500 clicks and <5 conversions after 14 days — would prevent wasted spend.
